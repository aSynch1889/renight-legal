Subscriptions

Cancel or manage a Renight Pro subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase. Restore Purchases from the app or paywall.

Wake reminders & alarms

On iOS 26+, Renight can schedule system-managed wake alarms via AlarmKit when authorized. On iOS 17–25 (or without AlarmKit authorization), Renight uses local notifications, and 30-minute pre-wake reminders are always local notifications. Silent mode, Focus, revoked permissions, or force-closing the app can affect local notifications or background audio transitions. Please also use the built-in iOS Clock alarm for critical schedules.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap Calibrate (auto-stopping after 15 seconds) to estimate relative room loudness. Audio is not recorded, stored, or uploaded.

Wellness only

Renight is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions. Its sounds, estimates, and journal statistics are informational.
