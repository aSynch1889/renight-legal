Last updated 18 November 2026 · Applies to the Renight iOS app (com.evolly.renight)

Wellness, not medical care

Renight is a wellness sound, journaling, and education app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Brainwave sounds, sleep-cycle estimates, breathwork pacing, and articles are informational only. Talk to a qualified clinician if you have a sleep disorder or other health concern.

Subscriptions and payments

Optional Renight Pro access is sold as auto-renewable subscriptions and/or a lifetime unlock, billed by Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Unused portions of a free trial, if offered, are forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

Apple charges your Apple ID within the 24 hours before the current subscription period ends. Lifetime Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase.

Current prices, trial length, and billing period are shown in the App Store product page and in the in-app paywall via StoreKit. They may vary by storefront.

Alarms and background audio

On iOS 26 and later, when you grant Alarm permission, wake alarms are scheduled through Apple’s AlarmKit system alarm service. On iOS 17–25 (or if AlarmKit permission is declined), wake reminders fall back to local notifications, and 30-minute pre-wake reminders always use local notifications. If the app is force-quit, iOS will not start background sleep-to-wake audio transitions automatically. Do not rely on Renight as a medical alarm or sole wake-up device.
