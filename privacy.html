Privacy

What Renight keeps — and what it never takes.

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Renight iOS & iPadOS app (com.evolly.renight)

What stays on your device

Sleep journal entries, Bedtime Thought Vault notes and to-dos, saved custom soundscape mixes, configured wake reminders, personal sleep goal, language, appearance, and onboarding preferences are stored locally on your device using system preferences (UserDefaults). Bedtime questionnaire choices are evaluated in memory only. None of this personal routine data is uploaded to a Renight account server, and we never sell personal data.

Microphone

If you tap Calibrate, Renight uses the microphone for up to 15 seconds only to estimate relative room loudness in real time so playback masking volume can be adjusted. Audio is never written to disk and is never uploaded. Stopping calibration (or leaving the screen) immediately releases the microphone.

Purchases & Subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are billed by Apple through StoreKit 2. Renight uses RevenueCat solely as purchase and entitlement infrastructure to validate subscription status and restore purchases across devices using an anonymous app user identifier and purchase history. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store billing, and RevenueCat processes purchase metadata strictly for app functionality.

Analytics and ads

Renight does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. Data is never used for third-party targeted advertising.
