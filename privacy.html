Privacy

What Renight keeps — and what it never takes.

Last updated 20 August 2026 · Applies to the Renight iOS app (com.evolly.renight)

What stays on your device

Sleep logs, bedtime questionnaire answers, language, appearance, mixer levels, and similar preferences are stored locally on the device. We do not operate a Renight account server and we do not sell personal data.

Microphone

If you tap Calibrate, Renight uses the microphone only to estimate room loudness in real time so playback volume and masking suggestions can be adjusted. Audio is not written to disk and is not uploaded. Stopping calibration releases the microphone.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app reads entitlement status on device. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store billing.

Analytics and ads

This version of Renight does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the App Store listing for Renight.
